Features

Everything between here and there

Built on open transit data and a real trip planner, so one search covers the whole city.

Buses and metro together

Walk, BMTC bus and Namma Metro legs combined into a single itinerary with estimated fares.

Trains included

Bengaluru train options come from a nightly timetable, joined to bus and metro legs at each station.

One-tap auto handoff

Open Namma Yatri, Uber or Ola, whichever you have installed, for the first or last mile.

No sign-up, no payments

No account, no ads and no tickets sold. You pay the operator or ride app directly.

How it works

From address to ride in three steps

Plan once, then jump straight into the app you already use.

Search

Enter where you are and where you are going, or use your location.

Compare

Pick an itinerary mixing walking, bus, metro and train.

Go

Tap the auto option to continue the first or last mile in your ride app.

Times and fares are estimates from timetable data, not live vehicle positions, unless a departure is marked live.
