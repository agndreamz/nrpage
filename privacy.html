Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

NammaRoute is a trip-search app for Bengaluru. This policy explains what the app handles when you use it. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the app.

What the app uses

  • Location. If you allow location permission, your approximate or precise location is used to set your starting point and centre the map. You can deny it and type addresses instead.
  • Places you search for. The text you type in the search boxes, and the origin and destination of a trip, are sent to our server to find matching places and plan the journey.
  • Details kept on your phone. Your theme, ride-app choices, saved places (home, work, airport), recent searches, and any name or phone number you enter are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes them.

Who receives data

  • NammaRoute server (AWS, India region). Receives trip searches and place queries to return itineraries. Standard server and network logs, such as IP address, time and request path, may be kept for operations and abuse prevention.
  • Ola Maps. Address search and place suggestions are forwarded through our server to Ola Maps. The Ola key stays on the server; your device identifiers are not passed on.
  • Google Maps SDK. The map is drawn by Google Maps, which receives data under Google's privacy policy.
  • Ride apps. When you tap an auto option, the app opens Namma Yatri, Uber or Ola on your phone and passes the pickup and drop locations. What happens next is governed by that app's own policy. NammaRoute does not see your booking or payment.
  • Train timetables. Train results come from a timetable stored on our server; your search is not sent to a train service.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not process payments or store payment cards. Fares shown are estimates.
  • We do not build a profile of you across apps.

Retention

Trip searches are used to answer your request. Server logs are kept only as long as needed for operations and security, then deleted.

Your choices

  • Deny or revoke location permission in Android settings; the app still works with typed addresses.
  • Clear the app's storage to remove everything kept on your phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date if the app's data handling changes. Questions or deletion requests: email supportavir@gmail.com.
