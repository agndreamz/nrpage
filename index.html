NammaRoute

Trip search for Bengaluru across BMTC buses, Namma Metro and trains, with a one-tap handoff to an auto for the first and last mile.

Buses and metro, together

One plan combines walking, BMTC bus and Namma Metro legs, built on OpenTripPlanner and community GTFS feeds.

Trains from a nightly timetable

Bengaluru train options come from a timetable refreshed every night, and are added to the bus and metro legs around each station.

Auto handoff

Tap to open Namma Yatri, Uber or Ola, whichever is installed on your phone, for the first or last mile.

No payments here

NammaRoute does not sell tickets or take payments. You pay the operator or the auto platform directly.

How it works

  1. Enter where you are and where you are going.
  2. Pick an itinerary mixing walking, bus, metro and train.
  3. Tap the auto option to continue the first or last mile in your ride app.

Data

BMTC & Namma Metro

Community GTFS feeds from bmtc-gtfs and bmrcl-gtfs.

Streets

OpenStreetMap, clipped to the transit service area.

Fares and times are estimates from timetable data, not live vehicle positions, unless a departure is marked live.
